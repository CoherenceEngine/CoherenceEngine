Archived benchmark / local replay

Scania APS

85.7% fewer recorded heavy-model calls

Comparator: always-on Extra Trees workflow.

Outcome: 13,708 of 16,000 calls avoided in the recorded routing workflow. False positives fell from 841 to 740; missed failures remained at six.

Operational relevance: illustrates how selective routing could reduce evaluation workload while preserving the baseline’s detected failures in this ledger.

Call counts use recorded flags and an always-on baseline convention, not instrumented compute or physical energy measurements. Outside execution review remains pending.

Synthetic simulation / local reproduction

HBI v0.4.1

20.2% less modeled energy

Comparator: locked baseline control policy across 72 paired cases.

Outcome: modeled energy 80.230 → 63.997, 88.0% fewer command events, and 61.5% fewer active control steps. Both policies survived 72/72 cases with zero recorded violations.

Operational relevance: illustrates a possible tradeoff between control activity and modeled resource use.

Benchmark proxy operators, synthetic conditions, and local byte-exact reproduction. These results do not establish physical savings or performance of all protected engine operators.
