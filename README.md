# Coherence Engine™ · Allison Hensgen

**See what individual signals can't reveal.**

Coherence Engine™ is an implemented, benchmark-tested computational system for examining changing relationships, drift, instability, and recovery in dynamic systems. The public site connects technical evidence to potential decisions and applications, while distinguishing implemented results from research hypotheses.

### Experimental application: Coherence Sound™
The H0 prototype processed recorded camera/microphone-derived signals through the unchanged Coherence Engine™ and generated sound. Physiological interpretation, recovery detection, and robustness to acquisition artifacts remain under evaluation. Wearable and cross-organism interfaces are proposed, not demonstrated.
