Independent Validation Program

From reported results to third-party replication

This portal explains how Coherence Engine™ claims are being converted into frozen, reproducible validation protocols. The goal is not to present every experiment as a win. The goal is to let an independent reviewer determine what reproduces, what does not, and the operating conditions under which the engine adds value.

What independent replication can de-risk

Technical risk

Separates repeatable signal from development-set effects, comparator choice, or post-hoc thresholding.

Commercial risk

Identifies where early warning, compute gating, lower intervention burden, or energy effects are large enough to justify a pilot.

IP risk

Creates an auditable evidence package without turning the proprietary engine into an open-source disclosure.

Authorized reviewer workflow
01 · ACCESS

Reviewer is granted read access to the private repository.

02 · DOWNLOAD

Clone the repository or download the source archive from GitHub.

03 · VERIFY

Run environment and frozen-manifest checks before any engine execution.

04 · EXECUTE

Add canonical datasets and an authorized black-box CE build, then run the frozen protocol.

05 · REPORT

Generate an independent report preserving positive, mixed, negative, and failed runs.

Evidence boundary

Prior reported benchmark values are reference targets for replication, not independent validation. Modeled energy is not physical electricity. Preliminary plug-level measurements are not a universal energy-savings claim. A third-party result applies only to the tested dataset, hardware, workload, comparator, and frozen protocol.
