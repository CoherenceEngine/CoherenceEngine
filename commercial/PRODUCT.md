# The Coherence Engine™: controlled gating evaluation
Version: commercial-package-0.1.0 | Updated: 2026-10-13

## Turn selective evaluation into measurable value
Evaluate whether The Coherence Engine™ can reduce expensive model calls while preserving agreed detection performance on your data.

The initial offer is a scoped, licensed technical evaluation for teams operating costly classification or anomaly-detection pipelines. Likely buyers include ML platform leads and industrial analytics teams. These are target customers, not confirmed customers.

## Scope
- Inputs: agreed numeric feature records, documented units and missingness, an identified baseline model, and separately held outcome labels.
- Outputs: per-record routing ledger, paired baseline comparison, operational measurements, limitations, and a go/no-go report.
- Delivery: controlled batch evaluation first. Hosted API and packaged customer deployment require separate implementation and acceptance.
- Customer decision: whether to proceed to a monitored integration pilot.
- Exclusions: autonomous safety decisions, clinical decisions, guaranteed savings, and cross-domain performance guarantees.

## Evidence
The [Scania record](https://github.com/CoherenceEngine/Research/tree/main/validation/scania_v1) reports 85.7% fewer recorded heavy-model calls across 16,000 records, false positives reduced from 841 to 740, and six missed failures in both policies. These are dataset-specific recorded outcomes. They do not establish deployed savings or performance on customer data.

## Delivery boundary
The core engine, operators, thresholds, and calibration remain proprietary. Public examples demonstrate an integration contract only. Supported engine runtime, OS, hardware, and deployment mode must be established in the signed evaluation scope and release manifest.
