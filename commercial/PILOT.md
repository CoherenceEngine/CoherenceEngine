# Paid controlled evaluation
Version: 0.1.0 | Status: offer structure, delivery gates pending

## Establish value on an agreed decision
A proposed four-to-six-week evaluation begins after the data, executable package, commercial agreement, and evaluation protocol are ready. Timing is an estimate, not a delivery commitment.

| Stage | Work | Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope | Identify buyer decision, baseline, data rights, cost model, and pass/fail criteria | Signed statement of work and frozen protocol |
| Preparation | Validate schema, isolate labels, freeze engine and baseline versions | Artifact manifest and data-quality report |
| Evaluation | Paired held-out comparison and operational measurement | Decision ledger, metrics, uncertainty, and failures |
| Review | Assess safety, economics, and integration effort | Go/no-go report and next-stage proposal |

## Success criteria, agreed before testing
Record exact values for minimum call reduction, maximum excess missed-event rate, maximum false-alarm burden, maximum p95 latency, and minimum net value. No default success threshold is implied. If margins or cost assumptions are missing, the evaluation is not ready to start.
Use paired uncertainty estimates and the agreed non-inferiority rule. Zero observed additional misses does not establish zero future risk. Freeze sample-size rationale, holdout policy, event definitions, and stop conditions.

## Customer responsibilities
Provide authorized data, an identified baseline, compute access where agreed, reliable outcome labels, a technical contact, and documented business costs. Keep test labels unavailable to calibration. Confirm acceptable handling of sensitive data before transfer.

## Commercial structure
A fixed evaluation fee is quoted after scoping, based on data preparation, adapter work, compute, reviewer time, and reporting. The signed statement of work establishes amount, payment schedule, delivery dates, cancellation, acceptance, and permitted use. No published price or production SLA is committed by this document.

## Delivery acceptance
Acceptance covers delivery of the agreed artifacts and reproducible report, even when performance gates fail. A technical success gate determines progression, separately from contractual delivery acceptance. No automatic production license, exclusivity, or publication permission is granted.
