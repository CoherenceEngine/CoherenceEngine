# Support and maintenance
Status: proposed pilot policy, contractual terms pending

The initial point of contact is Allison Hensgen at CoherenceDashboard@gmail.com. Staffing capacity, backup ownership, and response times must be confirmed before a customer launch.

Each statement of work must name the technical owner, customer contact, escalation contact, support hours/timezone, incident response targets, and supported release. No 24/7 coverage, uptime guarantee, or production SLA is currently established by this package.

Pilot changes require a versioned release, impact review, regression checks, and customer notification. Do not silently change a frozen evaluation. Record compatibility, migration instructions, rollback, deprecation dates, and support end dates.

Before production, demonstrate incident handling and establish backup maintainership. Support commitments must match available staffing and validated operating capacity.
